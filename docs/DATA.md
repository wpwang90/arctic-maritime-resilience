This is a code-only release. Restore authorized inputs locally without committing
them. Root paths below are relative to `13_CMIP6_GridPath_Figures/`.

| Input | Expected location |
| --- | --- |
| AIS-derived physical network and canonical port demand | `data/physical_arctic_embedding/oxmartrans_1390/` |

| PortWatch daily series | `../10_Nature_RealData_Upgrade/data_public/portwatch/` |

No raw-data acquisition or historical port-repair pipeline is shipped. Begin from
the audited physical network and source records used for the final figures.
Use the retained route and climate functions to recompute their derived states.
The input-check runner tests a minimum set, not the entire transitive inventory.

Legacy external input constants use `ARCTIC_LOCAL_DATA` instead of a personal
absolute path:

```sh
export ARCTIC_LOCAL_DATA=/path/to/authorized/shipping-inputs
```

The expected relative names remain in the relevant script constants. The CMIP6
inventory must point to your actual NetCDF files with unchanged model, variable,
experiment, calendar and checksum identities.

## Procurement experiments

S24 and S25 share inputs in `71_Rank_Trade_Benefit_Experiment_20261111/`:
`market_inputs.csv`, `rank_inputs.csv`, `input_provenance.json`, the two
`policy_logits_*.jsonl.gz` files and matching `policy_provenance_*.json`.
The table preparation script reads the route-aligned OD loss and industry
bridge products and the numeric rank source table.

S25 additionally reads its `buyer_logits_*.jsonl.gz` and
`buyer_provenance_*.json` files. Generate them with `switch_market.py` and the
separately obtained checkpoints, or restore archived scored logs. These are
experimental prompt/score records, not chat history. They are not uploaded here.

Local inference uses Apple Silicon/Metal and the checkpoint revisions specified
in `market.py`. CPU market replay from scored logs requires neither the weights
nor a network API.

## Generated panels

The final dual-axis Main Figure 3 uses the vector panels generated by
`60_*/plot_partI_extreme_mainfig3.py`, with terminology relabelled by the
`64_*` helper. Its two numeric series come from the `60_*/results/` directory.
The `tools/final_artwork.py` helper prepares these non-manuscript inputs and
collects final numbered PDF outputs. It does not create captions or documents.

All third-party inputs remain subject to their own access and redistribution
terms. Missing inputs must not be replaced by artificial research observations.
